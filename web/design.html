Buttons button · .primary · .secondary · .ghost · .danger · .lg · .block · .busy

48 px minimum, 56 px with .lg (the bottom bar makes every button large). Pressing scales to 97%. .busy swaps the label for a spinner. Anchors take .button.

Anchor as .button

Forms .field · input · select · input[type=range] · .toggle · .stepper

52 px fields with 17 px text (iOS won't zoom). Range shows a filled track when JS sets --range-pct (this page does). The toggle is a plain button with aria-pressed: the switch is a fixed 42 × 26 flex item beside the label with a 12 px gap, so the knob never covers the text and long labels wrap next to it (.small is the 34 × 20 header variant). The stepper is two 48 px buttons around a count.
